# Decisions

31 October 2026.

1. Preserve the specification pack and earlier projects. Implement in the separate last_signal subdirectory. No application or Git repository was present here. Preserve the unrelated service on port 5173.
2. Start with Gates 0, 1 and the core of 2. Keep practice useful before multiplayer or chain work. No public hosting is attempted in this local first slice.
3. Use npm workspaces and a tracked package lock. Pin registry versions after checking official Vite, Three.js, Fastify and Vitest documentation. Node 24 satisfies their reported requirements.
4. Use a small vanilla TypeScript HUD instead of React for this slice. State stays in the simulation and Three.js owns its scene. React remains an option for future lobby and wallet views. Strict TypeScript is enabled throughout.
5. Use a fixed perspective camera, original generated ground texture, procedural buildings, rain line geometry and three soldier meshes per squad. These are temporary art. Color management uses sRGB output and ACES tone mapping.
6. Use stable four neighbor breadth first navigation search. It is sufficient on this authored grid and avoids grid corner cutting. Home protection is part of legal navigation.
7. Quantize movement to four decimal places each tick. Collect simultaneous volleys and resolve shield absorption sorted by attacker ID. Determinism is verified on this JavaScript runtime. Cross runtime hashing and a persistent replay journal are future work.
8. Bot input is an authorized observation. Budget, cooldown and spending validation use the same function as the player. Friendly squad overlap is allowed per the spec.
9. Hide enemy reinforcement announcements and live meshes outside sight. This is local presentation and bot fairness, not a verified multiplayer trust boundary. The browser owns the complete practice state.
10. Hidden tabs pause practice and drop accumulated render time. Catchup is bounded at five ticks per frame. A slow device slows practice instead of skipping combat. Network overload policies await an authoritative service.
11. Ship low violence presentation with no blood. Audio initially uses optional original synthesized command tones. Advanced effects and ambience remain unfinished.
12. Bundle three fonts and SIL Open Font License texts so practice does not rely on a font CDN. Sources and author notices are in the asset register and license files.
13. The owner rejected the distant board view and requested a much closer view resembling first person, more interaction and detailed action. This authorizes replacing the fixed camera choice in the original pack. Default to eye level with a visible procedural weapon. Retain squad and command views for navigation. WASD issues ordinary validated squad movement commands. Combat remains automatic and one squad remains one entity.
14. Use original articulated figures, weapon recoil, muzzle flashes, three cosmetic tracer bursts per authoritative volley, ejected casings, sparks, dust, footstep splashes, smoke and casualty falls. Cosmetic bursts never add damage. Hidden units that leave sight disappear without a death animation; a casualty requires an actual observed destruction event. Effects cap at 160 objects and six temporary casualty groups.
15. Quick combat drill is a labeled local fixture for immediate action. It pays both sides for two preparing reinforcements through normal commands, advances the legal seven second preparation, then places three squads on authored opposing positions. Normal practice still starts at home with one free squad. Never call the drill a standard match opening or funded multiplayer.
16. The two full bot simulation test timed out under host load at the default five seconds. Increase that scenario's timeout to 30 seconds. Keep all gameplay constants and assertions unchanged. This is a test execution budget, not a balance adjustment.
17. Limit renderer pixel ratio to 1.4, cache movement path geometry until the route changes, and refresh shadow maps every fourth render frame. These reduce unnecessary render work without changing simulation timing. Device benchmark targets remain unverified.
18. The owner requested an older war setting while keeping guns and existing interaction. Use a fictional early 20th century inspired front: mud, duckboards, sandbags, damaged brick buildings, timber doors, wooden stocked rifles, steel brim helmets, canvas packs and leather equipment. This is a visual setting, not a claim of historical authenticity. Shield and EMP remain abstract prototype mechanics.
19. Preserve the authored obstacle footprints and gameplay rules. The communication lanes are shallow visual dressing, not new excavated navigation geometry. Perimeter wire is outside legal bounds. Small crates, barrels and vegetation are cosmetic props, not new cover or damaging hazards. No real faction insignia is introduced.
20. Use PCFShadowMap directly after the installed renderer logged that PCFSoftShadowMap falls back to it. No dependency changes are required.

22. The owner could not locate squads or relays. Make squad cards cyan and explicitly named; give stations named destination buttons. Guided practice starts overhead, highlights the required control and offers its command inside the tutorial. Explain that a relay is a radio station inside a ground circle. Allow instructions to collapse. Retain the ordinary close camera and simulation rules.

23. Opening screen squad selection starts guided practice so it cannot leave a beginner in an apparently selected but unplayable standby state. Use one selection handler for cards, number keys, tutorial and world picks. Keep card DOM nodes stable during health updates. Give an explicit next action after selection; unavailable squad shortcuts preserve the current selection.

24. Make destination targeting explicit. MOVE TO… opens the overhead view and a persistent ground selection prompt. Hide the training panel temporarily while targeting so it cannot intercept the destination click. Accepted movement and direct relay orders clear targeting. Escape cancels targeting before deselecting a squad. Keep tutorial actions visible while longer explanations scroll.

25. Cancel unfinished targeting on selected squad loss and match completion. Announce automatic replacement selection so a beginner can understand why the camera or selected card changed. No squad left means no selected squad.


Player control revision, 31 October 2026: the owner explicitly requested mouse look, direct motion and manual firing while soldiers remain automatic. EYE LEVEL now offers TAKE CONTROL, mouse yaw and pitch to approximately 80 degrees up or down, left click or hold to fire a bolt action rifle, right hold to aim, and Escape to release. WASD uses validated direct movement with collision sliding, immediate stop on key release and a short input expiry. Strafe direction is corrected relative to camera right. The close camera no longer jumps above a building when the player stands beside a wall. Automatic squad volleys remain 10 damage per second; a player rifle shot adds 5 damage at most once per second within the existing 8 unit range. Both resolve in the fixed tick combat phase, with shield, cover, EMP, home safety and observation checks. The new rules version is practice-2-rifle. Shots aimed above or beside enemies miss. A ray stops at canonical wall volumes. Targets still use aggregate squad spheres, not individual soldier hitboxes. No ammunition or reload inventory is added. Rifle hits and target health appear in the HUD. No dependencies or external assets were added.


Untimed survival revision, 31 October 2026. Owner requested removal of the timer, main character death, meaningful damage, obvious enemy health, finite bullets, recoverable guns and WIF, and coordinated enemies. All web modes now create untimed practice games with a fixed main character. Timed regulation remains available only in the underlying legacy simulation mode and tests. The web run ends at 240 points, concession or main character death. NPC death drops a gun, 12 bullets and five simulated practice WIF; collection requires the living main character within two metres and line of sight. Duplicate collection cannot award twice. WIF is a separate local counter with no real value or chain read. Service rifle damage is 20 with six rounds, carbine damage is 12 with ten rounds and faster cadence. R reloads from finite reserve, G loots, X swaps collected types and T retreats. Shared ammo is conserved across switching. Automatic squad volleys remain 10 damage. Player health remains linked to the main character squad entity; individual soldier hitboxes are not implemented.

Use deterministic observation limited team tactics: shared focus targets, distinct flank positions, cover occupancy checks, defending captured relays, low health retreat and shields. Evasion physically moves the squad after incoming damage with a three second cooldown. It never cancels a hit that already landed or invents knowledge of hidden enemies. Guided teaching keeps its scripted route until training completes. Add a visible pause control and P shortcut for local practice inspection. Keep mouse trigger release robust across mouseup, pointerup, pointercancel, reload, hidden tabs and focus loss.

26. Provide a labelled, disarmed firing range for learning the real shooting and pickup loop without incoming damage. Normal command validation, finite ammo, damage and loot remain active; ordinary bot commands are paused only for that explicit fixture. Keep its result label and history metadata distinct from normal bot practice. START LIVE MATCH resets the run instead of carrying training pickups into combat.
27. Show pickup distance and interaction readiness on world markers. Add a reload progress bar and original procedural weapon lowering and bolt motion; reduced motion suppresses the larger view movement.


Squad survival balance, 31 October 2026. Rules practice 4. The main character takes half incoming damage and has a 0.5 second protection window after a hit to prevent simultaneous focused volleys stacking. Death still ends the run. Rifle: 20 damage, four shots per second, 20 loaded and 80 reserve. Carbine: 12 damage, about 6.7 shots per second, 30 loaded. Reload: 1.5 seconds. Reserve capacity: 180. Holding left click repeats shots and spends finite ammunition.

Squads lose one visible soldier at each third of health, helpers before the leader. Falling bodies remain at the casualty position for up to 12 seconds, bounded to 18 bodies. Casualties remain visible with combat effects disabled. Fog removal does not create bodies. Automatic support weakens with losses: main character helpers deal 10, then 5, then 0 per volley; other squads deal 10, then about 6.67, then about 3.33. Legacy timed simulation without a hero keeps its original volley damage. Health and hitboxes remain aggregated per squad; independent soldier targeting is not implemented.


Endless Front and recovery, 31 October 2026. Rules practice 5. The owner chose the action loop and practice WIF before multiplayer. Endless Front is an explicit local mode alongside classic relay practice and the disarmed range. Eliminate the wave, collect drops with G, recover during an eight second break and continue automatically. The first three waves grow from one to three enemy squads. Later waves keep three and shorten automatic volley cooldown to a minimum of 12 ticks, reached at wave nine. AI pursues observed opponents instead of defending owned stations or retreating permanently. Authored spawn positions remain legal and at least six units from the hero. Difficulty is capped; infinite procedural mission variety is not claimed.

Each clear awards ten practice WIF and 20 reserve bullets once. Ordinary enemy drops still award five WIF, a gun and 12 bullets when collected. H opens a paused, three choice supply panel: five WIF for 40 reserve bullets, five for a 30 capacity shield lasting four seconds, ten for one replacement helper and restored squad health after five seconds without damage. There are no real tokens, purchases, wallet balances, exchange prices or chain calls. Supplies spend the local WIF counter, separate from practice credits, with validation and recent command deduplication. WIF and inventory reset on explicit redeployment. Wave transitions preserve them. The local endless command cache is bounded and does not provide durable multiplayer deduplication.

Living friendly squads in hero practice regenerate eight HP per second after five seconds without health damage. A new damaging hit restarts the delay. Health caps at 100. A persistent member count prevents recovery from reviving fallen men or restoring their automatic fire. Replacements explicitly restore one missing helper, not a whole squad. Health, hitboxes and casualties still use aggregate squad simulation. Legacy timed matches without a hero do not heal.

The Endless Front first person HUD hides station orders and the planning deck; command view retains them. Relays are optional and reaching 240 signal does not end an endless run. Death or concession ends it. P remains a pause control; redeployment is explicit. No forced restart, daily obligation or loss of real assets is introduced. The delivery gates in NEXT_STAGES.md remain pending. No multiplayer or escrow work is claimed for this revision.
